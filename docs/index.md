# Sankhara

Sankhara follows a people from a small band at their first fires to whatever
they become, lived one person at a time. Each turn is a year. You act as one
character, with a few slots of attention and the means your place among the
people gives you: what is held in common if you lead, your family's if you
hunt, gather or keep the songs. Some things take years; events arrive, some of
them asking something of you.

The ideas behind it are in [Concepts](concepts.md); how they are built is in
[Architecture](architecture.md).

!!! note "Status"
    Playable: a band of a few hundred people, yearly turns, multi-year
    undertakings you can let run, a web of discoveries whose forms the people
    settle on, roles that appear as the people change, and other peoples to
    meet. Policy and more development come in Milestone 2; detailed NPC
    relationships and politics in Milestone 3.

## Running

```bash
uv sync
uv run sankhara        # or: uv run python -m sankhara
```

Choose **New Game**. Click a slot under *Your attention* to decide what the
year is for, then **End the Year**, or **Let the years pass** to keep going
until something needs you. The game saves itself every year; **Continue**
picks up where you left off.
