# Plan

What has been built, and what comes next. The design itself is in
[Concepts](concepts.md); play-test notes there are folded into this plan.

## Done

- **M1, the band**: yearly turns, three slots of attention, lives and the next
  life, hidden karma and inclinations, the people's works, earned leadership,
  the first web of ideas and their forms, other peoples.
- **M2, policy and government**: a second era of ideas (the plough to the town),
  customs that can be settled anew as a people's work, how the people decide
  governing who settles customs, what is owed to the leader, those who fight.
- **M3, people and politics** (first pass): who is around you and how they feel,
  feuds between known families and grudges that outlive them, rivals who ask the
  people to follow them, factions and a divided people, matches and marriages
  between known families, kin by marriage who ask for help and give it.

## M4: choices that fit

From the play-test notes: choices should match the role, grow with the society,
and not all be good.

1. **Role and scale** (begun: who has a say, strangers and newcomers, judging, each food role's own matter). Each matter is put to the people it would really reach.
   In a band anyone may take a stranger in; in a village the leader decides; once
   there is a council or judgement, the elders or a judge do; in a town it is no
   one's single choice, and the matter becomes something else (newcomers settling,
   traders at the market). Hunters meet hunters' matters, keepers keepers'. The
   size of the people and what they know set which matters arise at all.
2. **Harm you can start** (built: theft, fights, affairs, killing, blood owed, lies, short measure). Under "your own", deeds that do harm: take from
   another family's stores, start a fight, spread a lie about someone, an affair,
   cheat in an exchange, and, at the far end, kill a rival. They can work, may be
   found out, and bring grudges, feuds, exile or revenge. Weighed by intention,
   like everything else; never labelled.
3. **Neither good nor bad** (built: bride-price, captives, a price for the wrong, ordeal, keeping seed back). Choices that are ordinary in the people's own terms
   but sit uneasily by ours: blood-price instead of judgement, ordeal, taking
   captives after a raid, a bride-price, keeping seed back from the hungry for
   next year's planting.
4. **Choices that grow with what the people know** (built: lending and debts with a market, false marks with writing, levies for the wall, the leader's share). Each later idea brings new
   deeds and matters for the roles it touches: debts and lending with a market,
   records (and false ones) with writing, levies of labour with walls, collecting
   (and skimming) tribute.

## M5: seeing the people (next)

1. **The web of ideas as a graph** (built): nodes and edges, the known and those the
   people are close to (faint, with their hint); nothing beyond, so nothing is
   spoiled. Laid out by depth, so it shows how far along the people are.
2. **The map as the main view of the people**: villages as places, tribes as
   regions containing them, the nation as the whole; zooming out moves from the
   person to the people. Where your kin, allies and rivals live is drawn on it,
   and where your last life stood, so a new life can be placed against the old.
3. **A small graph centred on you**: close family and the few with strong ties
   (friends, rivals, enemies), village and tribe as outlines around them, how
   each feels shown by line and distance rather than words or numbers. Choosing
   a person on the map shows their ties; choosing a place in the graph goes to it
   on the map.

Tribes do not yet exist in the simulation; M5 adds them as clusters of villages
that share descent (camps founded from one another), unless the design says
otherwise.
