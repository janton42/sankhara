    Architecture¶

    All code lives in the sankhara package under src/. The game is split into a pure-Python simulation (sankhara.sim, no Kivy, fully unit-tested and saveable) and a UI (sankhara.ui) that only reads state and calls a handful of game functions.

    Package Responsibility
    sankhara.sim The game: state, rules, the yearly loop, lives and save/load.
    sankhara.world Map generation (map_gen.py) and hex-grid math (hexgrid.py).
    sankhara.core game_engine.generate_map(seed) builds the terrain.
    sankhara.entities Name generation per culture (namer.py).
    sankhara.ui Kivy screens: menu, game view (with the chooser, "What we know" and the choice of a custom's form), between-lives screen; theme.py palette and widgets; words.py turns numbers into the words the player sees.
    sankhara.constants config.py: map size, noise, biome thresholds and resources, culture groups.
    sankhara.app SankharaApp: screen flow and autosave.

    How the design maps to code¶

    The design lives in Concepts. After the first play-through the game starts with a small band rather than a kingdom; this is how that is built:

    Design Implementation
    One turn is one year game.end_year() advances the world one year, then begin_year() sets up the next.
    2–3 slots of personal attention stations.ACTION_SLOTS = 3. game.options() offers three groups (Option.group): matters at hand (each EventInstance with answers, action ids open to the player's role; events.offer() fills them and marks the matter expected when the role has a plain duty in it), the people's works (projects.py; one of each kind of new work, aimed where it is needed most) and your own (personal projects and the lasting cares, Action.care). Nothing is chosen for the player: slots start free, a care is kept up until changed, an answer is kept up while its matter recurs (plan keys a:<matter key>:<action> are stable across years), and a project's slot goes free when it ends (game.begin_year, replan).
    Lead from wherever you are Most projects are the people's works (Project.common). Finishing one earns its leader and helpers regard (FINISH_REGARD, HELPED_REGARD), so serving the people is the road to leading them. Anyone can begin one with their own means (at half cost; slower; camp works only at their own camp unless they lead), help with one someone else leads (each helper adds work), take up one left unfinished, or call others to it (rally: spends standing to add support, which adds work and draws NPC helpers). NPCs join works more readily when the one leading is respected or liked. When a leader dies or sets a work aside it passes to a helper, the people's leader or an elder (projects.pass_on), or waits for someone. A custom being settled lets anyone speak for a form; a respected voice weighs more (game.choose_form, pending_mode).
    The thread between lives Works an earlier character of the player's worked on stay in play: the birth screen lists what was finished while the new character grew up and what is still unfinished and who carries it (game.thread_lines), and the chooser marks those works. GameState.legacy remembers finished works and settled customs the player had a hand in; the end-of-life screen shows them.
    Years that run on their own "Let the years pass" runs a clock (GameView._tick, PACES): a year lasts a few seconds, shown by a bar, so there is time to pause. News (YearReport.interrupts: something expected of you, a finished or stalled project, a discovery, strangers, a death or birth) is shown with its year and makes that year last longer (NEWS_LINGER), but doesn't stop the years. Only a pop-up (a custom to decide or speak for) or a window the player opens holds the year; a life ending shows the life screen, and the years resume after it. A Project keeps its slot year after year until finished, set aside or stalled.
    Attending to the years, or letting them slip game.end_year(attended=...): a year the player steps through weighs slightly well (ATTENDED_KARMA); a year let pass with nothing taken up weighs slightly ill (DRIFTED_KARMA); a year let pass while working at something is neutral. Hidden like all karma.
    A coarse budget, no money at the start Budget is open-ended: food, hands (labour, renewed yearly) and regard (standing). The leader also draws on what is held in common. Exchange adds whatever form the people settle on (favours owed, wares, or tokens named for the land: shells, polished stones, beads). Costs for the common stores scale with the square root of the population.
    Start as the first band, not a civilisation game.new_game(): about 250 people in three camps, four known things (fire, stone edges, the old songs, a belief in the unseen whose form is random), and a first character the people have lately come to look to, for a reason drawn at random (bringing the hunters home through the snows, driving off a bear, settling a quarrel, finding water). Their predecessor gave way or died; nothing is inherited.
    Development like Civilization, but not prescribed discoveries.py: about 45 ideas in four domains (land, craft, lore, kin), in two eras. The first thirty or so (fire to planting, herding, a council, exchange, judgement) take 200–300 years; the later ones (LATER_IDEAS: the plough, copper, the wheel, walls, writing, tribute, those who fight, a market, the town...) need those first, and more people or trouble from outside, so they come over the next 150–200 years. One is available only when its prerequisites and circumstances hold (water nearby, grassland, hills, having met another people, an unfriendly neighbour, enough people). Insight comes from ordinary life every year, from someone working at it (the pursue project, one idea at a time), from chance events, and from other peoples. Walls and those who fight add defence, which makes raids rarer; once the people know what a town is, villages above TOWN_SIZE become towns.
    Ideas settle into forms Discoveries like customs of giving, planting, exchange, how leaders are chosen, or judgement have several forms with different effects (Form). The player chooses if they worked it out, or if they lead and it is a matter of custom. Otherwise the people's own inclinations decide. A choice strengthens its tags and weighs (lightly) on karma.
    Policy: customs can be settled anew A custom that has stood RESETTLE_AFTER years can be questioned (discoveries.customs). "Change how the people ..." is a people's work (reform) anyone respected can lead, help with or call others to; NPCs take it up mostly when the people are restless. When it is done the custom comes up again (GameState.due_choices, settled in game._settle_customs_anew): the player decides if they led the work or lead the people (and it is a matter of custom), otherwise speaks for a form; during a childhood the people's leanings decide, weighed by whoever called for it. discoveries.resettle undoes what the old form set once (a title, the rule of succession, a kind of exchange) before applying the new.
    Roles appear as the people change stations.py: leader, keeper, hunter, gatherer and maker at first; fisher, planter, herder and go-between appear with the discoveries that make them; hunters and gatherers grow rarer as herding and planting arrive. Rebirth draws from the roles that exist.
    Leadership is earned people.next_leader() chooses among the most respected (standing: regard, plus a little for elders), weighted toward the top, with no favour for the old leader's kin. While a leader lives, leadership_year() lets them give way when old or frail, or lets the people turn to someone who far outshines them. Anyone respected enough can also ask the people to follow them (the stand_forward deed); it may fail and cost standing. Later the form of "how the people decide" can change this: a council of elders, the strongest, or a lineage (the only form that ties leading to a family). Titles follow (nation.names).
    Government: how the people decide, and what they owe The form of "how the people decide" also settles who settles customs (game._decides, _voice, discoveries.deciders): with no custom, the leader decides matters of kin and custom; under a council of elders nobody decides alone, the elders' own leanings settle it and an elder's or the leader's word carries COUNCIL_VOICE times further; where the strongest leads, the chief settles every custom and other voices carry half. "What is owed to the leader" (tribute) feeds the leader's family (leader_food, scaled with the people) as well as the common stores.
    Other peoples; violence real but not the focus peoples.py: three other peoples, simulated coarsely. They grow, learn, meet you, share ideas, intermarry, and drift toward or away from you. Raids come as matters at hand: the leader is expected to seek redress; avenging is a harsh other answer, and anyone can help the struck camp rebuild. No armies: only once the people settle "those who fight" as a band of young men does a warrior role exist (stations: one who fights), with its own cares (keeping watch, which turns raids back for a year via nation.watched_until; training the young), answers (standing guard over a struck camp) and matters (a champion's challenge from another people).
    Karma only on the action layer Answers, cares and projects carry a hidden karma for their intention; an expected matter left unanswered subtracts EventInstance.penalty. Outcomes never touch it. Stored on Soul.karma, clamped to [-1, 1].
    Lives of 20–50 turns, random death age Character.death_age is hidden: 16 + 20..50. Health can end a life sooner.
    Rebirth among the same people, random role game.begin_next_life(); childhood is fast-forwarded to age 16.
    Karma shapes conditions, not rank Carried-over inclinations, health, fortune, family stability, parents' warmth and which family you are born to. Half of it is spent doing so.
    Tendencies, felt not shown tendencies.py: choosing strengthens tags; setting a project aside weakens them. Inclinations change costs (±30%), and order and tint in the chooser.
    NPCs, same model simplified npc.py: tracked adults take on projects (elders and the leader always) and a deed a year by inclination, chosen from their cares and the answers to the matters the people's state raises (events.lasting_answers: hunger, quarrels, low stores, plenty); the leader also weighs the people's needs. Their deeds earn standing; only the leader's act on the people.
    Relationships and politics (M3, begun) Everyone tracked has an affinity toward others; the panel's Around you (game.around_you) shows whoever leads and those in your camp you have most to do with, and how they feel about you in words (words.feeling_word). Feuds are between families you know (events._feud picks tracked families in your camp): siding with one wins its members and estranges the other's, and a feud that turns bloody kills a named man. A rival (events.rival: the most respected grown person near the leader's standing) is a matter for the leader (win them over, give them a place beside you, or face them down, a contest of standing) and for everyone else (speak for the rival, stand behind the leader, or keep out of it); unanswered, the talk grows and draws the rival's camp toward them. Ambition acts: each year the most respected of those the people talk of leading may ask the people to follow them (npc.would_bid, _bid; the chance rises with their ambition). Whether the people follow (people.bid_to_lead, also behind the player's "ask the people to follow you") weighs each side's standing and that of the grown people who would follow them rather than the other (people.support, by how everyone feels toward the two); a failed bid costs standing. Grudges outlive a feud: a feud left unhealed is remembered between the two families (Nation.grudges, for GRUDGE_YEARS unless renewed): their members are colder to each other (people.feels, used wherever feeling matters: who helps whose works, who would follow whom, how those around you feel), an old grudge is what most feuds flare from (events._feud), judgement or peacemaking ends it, and children are born with a part of their parents' friendships and grudges (INHERITED_FEELING). A new life's birth lines name the families its own has not spoken with. Factions: everyone who would follow one person rather than another is their faction (people.faction). While more of the people's weight stands behind a rival than behind the leader, the people are divided (events.divided_year, Nation.divided): cohesion frays each year, most in the rival's camp, with news when it begins and ends. Those who stood by a leader who is unseated resent whoever unseated them (LOYAL_RESENTMENT). Beside each person in Around you, game.ties adds an old grudge between their family and yours, and which side they take while a rival vies to lead.
    No win condition The game runs until the player stops; it autosaves every year.

    Simulation modules¶

    • state.py: dataclasses (GameState, Nation, Settlement, Neighbour, Project, Character, Soul, Budget, EventInstance) with to_dict/from_dict. The RNG state is saved too, so a loaded game plays on identically.
    • game.py: founding the people, the options for a year, the yearly loop and its interrupts, choosing a custom's form, death and the next life.
    • discoveries.py: the web of ideas, their forms and combined effects, yearly insight, and settling a custom anew.
    • projects.py: multi-year undertakings: working at ideas, ranging further, new camps, gathering places, store pits, fields, teaching, changing a custom, raising a child, apprentices, friendship and trade with other peoples...
    • actions.py: one-year deeds: lasting cares for each role, and the answers to matters at hand.
    • events.py: news and matters at hand (what lasts with the people's state, what comes by chance, what each role meets), their answers, yearly generation and resolution.
    • nation.py: each camp's food from its land (falling as it fills up), hunger and growth, what goes into the common stores, cohesion (which strains as the people grow), plenty and lore; each tracked family's means.
    • people.py: creating characters, families, ageing, marriage (sometimes to other peoples) and births, finding a new leader, keeping a few elders.
    • peoples.py, stations.py, tendencies.py, npc.py, world.py (cached map per seed, foraging values, place names), save.py (JSON).

    World generation¶

    1. Perlin noise produces elevation and moisture grids of MAP_WIDTH × MAP_HEIGHT, sampled at each hex's true position and normalised to [0, 1]. The game seed fixes the map.
    2. Each value is bucketed by ELEVATION_THRESHOLDS / MOISTURE_THRESHOLDS; the pair names the biome (e.g. "Hill Forest").
    3. Resources are seeded from BIOME_RESOURCE_BASELINES; a tile's fertility (plant food, animal food, fresh water) drives harvests.

    Hex grid¶

    world/hexgrid.py is pure math for a pointy-top grid in "odd-r" offset coordinates: centres, exact pixel-to-tile picking, corners, neighbours, distance and fitting a map to a box. The map view dims land outside the people's range, outlines its border, marks camps (the leader's in saffron), outlines the player's home, and shows the ranges of other peoples once met.
